      How to use the currency converter

      1. Enter the amount you want to convert. You can use a dot or a comma for decimals (1,250.50 or 1250,50).
      2. In “From”, type the country you are converting from, for example “United States”. You don't need to know the name of its currency: picking the country selects it automatically (in this case, the US dollar).
      3. In “To”, type the destination country, for example “Japan”, and you'll instantly see how many yen you get.
      4. Press Swap to do the reverse conversion, or share the result on WhatsApp or with a link.

      If you prefer, you can also search by currency name (“yen”, “pound”) or by its three-letter international code (“JPY”, “GBP”).

      Where do the exchange rates come from, and how precise are they?

      The converter uses the mid-market exchange rate: the midpoint between the buy and sell prices of each currency on international markets. It is the benchmark used by financial media and the fairest one for comparisons. Rates are refreshed daily and we always show you when they were last updated.

      Calculations use exact decimal arithmetic, not the approximate numbers many web calculators rely on. That is why the result is right to the cent; we also show you the unrounded value and the rate in both directions (for example, 1 EUR in USD and 1 USD in EUR).

      Keep in mind that when you pay by card or exchange cash, your bank or the exchange office will apply its own margin. Use this converter to know what something should cost and to spot when you're being overcharged. A tip: if a card terminal abroad offers to charge you “in your home currency”, it is usually more expensive — always choose the local currency.

      Which currency is used in each country?

      It isn't always obvious. The euro is the currency of 21 European Union countries, including Spain, France, Italy, Germany, Croatia and, since 2026, Bulgaria. It is also used in Andorra, Monaco, San Marino, Vatican City, Montenegro and Kosovo. Meanwhile, other EU members such as Czechia, Poland, Hungary, Sweden and Denmark keep their own currencies.

      The US dollar is the official currency not only of the United States but also of Ecuador, El Salvador and Puerto Rico, and it circulates alongside the balboa in Panama. In Africa, fourteen countries share the CFA franc in its two versions (West and Central). That's why this converter starts from the country: you type where you're going and we handle the rest.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is this currency converter free?

      Yes. Use it as often as you like, with no sign-up and nothing to install. It works on phones, tablets and computers.

      How often are the exchange rates updated?

      Rates are refreshed at least once a day from market sources. Below every result you will always see the date and time of the rates being used.

      Why does my bank give me a different amount?

      We use the mid-market rate, the most neutral benchmark. Banks, cards and exchange offices usually add a margin or a fee, so their result tends to be slightly worse.

      Can I convert between two currencies that are not the euro or the dollar?

      Yes. You can convert directly from any currency to any other, for example from Colombian pesos to Japanese yen or from Czech koruna to pounds.

      What happens to my data when I convert?

      Nothing: the calculation runs in your own browser. The amounts you type are never sent to or stored on any server.
