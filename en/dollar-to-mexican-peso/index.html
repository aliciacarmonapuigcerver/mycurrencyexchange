      Dollars to pesos: remittances, travel and shopping

      The United States and Mexico share one of the busiest borders in the world, and sending money from one country to the other is one of the largest remittance corridors on the planet. That's why millions of people check every day how many Mexican pesos (MXN) their dollars are worth.

      One thing that causes confusion: in Mexico prices are also written with the “$” sign, just like in the US. To tell them apart, the codes MXN and USD are used, or “MX$” and “US$”. If you see a price with “$” in Mexico, it is almost always in pesos.

      Tips for sending or exchanging dollars

      • Compare the exchange rate, not just the fee. A “no fee” service can give you noticeably fewer pesos than the market rate. Use this tool to know how many should arrive.
      • In tourist and border areas dollars are accepted, but the rate applied is usually worse than paying in pesos.
