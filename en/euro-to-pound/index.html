      Euros to pounds for travelling or studying in the UK

      The pound sterling (GBP) is the currency of the United Kingdom and one of the oldest still in use. Although the UK was part of the European Union until 2020, it never adopted the euro. The same goes for places such as Gibraltar, Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man, which issue their own pound notes worth exactly one pound sterling.

      Watch out in Ireland: the Republic of Ireland uses the euro, but Northern Ireland (part of the UK) uses the pound. If your trip crosses the border, you'll need both currencies.

      Practical tips

      • In the UK almost everything is paid by card or phone, including London public transport. A card with no foreign-transaction fees is usually the cheapest option.
      • Scottish and Northern Irish banknotes are widely accepted in the UK but can be hard to exchange abroad. Spend them before you leave.

      Can I pay with euros in the UK?

      Generally not. A few tourist shops accept them, but at a poor rate. Paying in pounds is better.
