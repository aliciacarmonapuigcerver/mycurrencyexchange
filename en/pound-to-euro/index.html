      Pounds to euros: the British traveller's conversion

      Spain, France, Portugal, Italy and Greece are among the favourite destinations for UK travellers, and many Britons own a second home or receive a pension in pounds while living in the eurozone. For all of them, the pound-euro rate decides how far their money goes each month.

      This tool tells you how many euros you get for your pounds at the mid-market rate. If you receive a pension or salary in pounds, compare this figure with what lands in your account to see the real cost of the transfer.

      Worth knowing

      • Small moves, big differences: on transfers of thousands of pounds, a fraction of a cent in the rate can mean tens of euros.
      • In the eurozone, always pay in euros when the card terminal asks. If you choose pounds, the shop applies its own rate, usually a worse one.

      Frequently asked questions

      How many euros is 100 pounds?

      Type 100 in the amount box to see the exact result. It also appears in the table of common amounts.

      Why doesn't the result match my bank's?

      Because banks usually add a margin to the mid-market rate. The difference is what you pay for the exchange.
