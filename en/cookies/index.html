Cookie policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) are small files or pieces of data a website stores on your device to remember information. This policy complies with Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI) and the cookie guidance of the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

2. First-party storage (technical, no consent required)

This site sets no first-party cookies. It only uses browser storage for technical purposes needed to provide the service you request:

3. Third-party cookies (require your consent)

We use Google AdSense, by Google Ireland Limited, to show ads. Google and its partners may set cookies and identifiers to serve ads, personalise them to your interests, cap how often you see an ad and measure their effectiveness. These cookies are only used if you give your consent in the message shown when you arrive, managed by Google's certified consent management platform (compliant with the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework).

You can see the list of advertising partners and their purposes in the message itself (“Manage options”), and find more information in how Google uses cookies in advertising.

4. How to change or withdraw your consent

  • Click “Cookie settings” in the footer of any page to reopen the consent message and change your choice.
  • You can also delete or block cookies in your browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.

5. Changes

We will update this policy if the cookies we use change. For more about how your data is handled, see the Privacy policy.
